dynamics.  Reciprocal symmetry, normalization, the recognition
composition law, and unit second-order calibration force a unique cost
functional, $\J(x)=\half(x+x^{-1})-1$.  The same functional forces a

target, with the Schwarzschild sector worked at residual $O(h^4)$, the

refinement schedules that derives it from each slice's own local cubic
bound, and the general recovery thereby reduced to a single named
analytic input, the cross-cardinality quadrature limit, which in turn
is closed outright on the flat sector and reduced, for uniform-probe
families, to a scalar limit of graph-Dirichlet energies.  In the high-frequency limit the substrate is
quantum mechanics rather than merely compatible with it: the
eight-tick register carries a forced complex structure, the Born weight

entropy with leading-log coefficient $c_\RS=-\half\log\phiG$ and a
finite Page curve that provably rises, peaks at $\half S_\BH$ at
half-evaporation, and falls.  The earlier ringdown-echo mechanism is

behind an event horizon does not by itself return a wavepacket to the

\section{Introduction: Gravity as the Comparison Channel}
\label{sec:intro}

Two problems drive the field.  The first is Planck-scale
incompleteness: general relativity predicts its own breakdown at
curvatures of order $\ell_\Pl^{-2}$, and no experiment has accessed
that regime.  The second is the absence of a measured quantum
gravitational degree of freedom.  Without data, ``quantize gravity'' is
ambiguous: which degrees of freedom carry the quantum structure, what
the Hilbert space is, and what dynamics acts on it are all open.  The
two problems compound.  The regime where quantum corrections matter is
precisely the regime where no measurement exists.

Five programs dominate.  Each brings real machinery, and each leaves
free parameters.

\paragraph{Loop quantum gravity.}
Ashtekar's connection variables~\cite{AshtekarLewandowski2004} lead to
a kinematic Hilbert space of spin networks with discrete area and
volume spectra.  The area gap scales with the Barbero-Immirzi parameter
$\gamma$~\cite{RovelliSmolin1995}, a free constant calibrated by
matching Bekenstein-Hawking entropy.  The kinematics is rigorous; the
spin-foam dynamics carries ambiguities under active investigation.  The
leading-log entropy coefficient in the standard counting is
$c_{\mathrm{LQG}}=-1/2$~\cite{KaulMajumdar2000}, contingent on the
$\gamma$ fixing.

\paragraph{String theory.}
The closed-string spectrum contains a massless spin-two excitation
reproducing linearized gravity, and Strominger-Vafa matched the
Bekenstein-Hawking entropy exactly for five-dimensional BPS black
holes~\cite{StromingerVafa1996}.  Beyond BPS the match is incomplete,
and the moduli space of vacua ($\sim 10^{500}$ by flux counting)
introduces adjustable structure that no selection principle has
removed.  For the Schwarzschild black hole, string theory does not
predict the leading-log coefficient.

\paragraph{Causal dynamical triangulations.}
CDT builds a non-perturbative path integral from causal simplicial
blocks~\cite{AmbjornJurkiewiczLoll2004}.  The emergent geometry shows a
spectral-dimension flow to $d_s\approx 2$ in the ultraviolet.  The
lattice couplings ($\kappa_0$, $\kappa_4$, $\Delta$) are free inputs
tuned to reach the physical phase.  No CDT prediction exists for the
entropy coefficient, the vacuum density, or the equation of state.

\paragraph{Asymptotic safety.}
Reuter's program~\cite{Reuter1998} posits a non-trivial ultraviolet
fixed point of the gravitational renormalization group.  Functional RG
evidence supports a fixed point with finitely many relevant directions,
but trajectory selection absorbs at least two data, and truncation
schemes move the fixed-point coordinates.

\paragraph{Causal sets.}
Spacetime as a locally finite partial
order~\cite{BombelliLeeMeyerSorkin1987} shares our commitment to
discreteness.  But the sprinkling is random rather than forced, and the
cosmological constant arises stochastically, as $\pm\sqrt{N}$
fluctuations, not as a single number.

Each program leaves at least one dial.  None produces a zero-parameter
prediction for the leading-log entropy coefficient, the vacuum energy
density, or the dark-energy equation of state.

\paragraph{This paper.}
Gravity is not a separate field to be quantized.  It is the
long-wavelength encoding of recognition imbalance on a substrate forced
by the symmetry of comparison, and quantum mechanics is the
phase-sensitive update of the same substrate.  We do not start from a
smooth metric and quantize it, and we do not start from a Hilbert space
and graft geometry onto it.  The comparison law is the primitive.
Curvature is the continuum bookkeeping of comparison mismatch.  The
amplitude channel is the same ledger read before the density
projection.  Quantum gravity is the ledger before either limit is
taken.

\section{The Unique Cost of Comparison}
\label{sec:cost}

\begin{definition}[Recognition cost]
For a positive ratio $x>0$, the recognition cost is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:J}
\J(x)=\frac{1}{2}\left(x+x^{-1}\right)-1=\cosh(\log x)-1 .
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

The cost measures how far a ratio sits from unity.  In log-coordinates
$t=\log x$ it is $\cosh t-1$: even, non-negative, convex, vanishing
only at $t=0$.

Four conditions force $\J$.  Each has a physical reading, and each is
independently necessary.

\paragraph{Condition 1: reciprocal symmetry.}
$F(x)=F(x^{-1})$.  Comparing $x$ to $1$ costs the same as comparing
$1/x$ to $1$.  The labeling of the two comparison partners is a
convention, and the cost cannot depend on it.  In log-coordinates this
is evenness.

\paragraph{Condition 2: normalization.}
$F(1)=0$.  Exact match carries zero cost.  Without this, an arbitrary
constant offset survives and uniqueness fails.

\paragraph{Condition 3: the recognition composition law.}
When two local comparisons with defects $u$ and $v$ compose, the
resulting defect is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rcl}
\mathcal{R}(u,v)=2uv+2u+2v .
\end{equation}
This polynomial is not chosen.  Among symmetric combiners
($\mathcal{R}(u,v)=\mathcal{R}(v,u)$) with right-affine structure
($\mathcal{R}(u,0)=2u$) and natural boundary ($\mathcal{R}(0,0)=0$),
it is the unique gate.

\begin{remark}[Higher-order terms are killed, not excluded]
\label{rem:right-affine}
The right-affine condition is not an a priori restriction to linear
combiners.  Suppose $\mathcal{R}(u,0)=2u+au^2+bu^3+\cdots$ with the
coefficients to be determined.  Compatibility with the d'Alembert-type
functional equation $f(s+t)+f(s-t)=\mathcal{R}(f(s),f(t))$ forces
$a=b=\cdots=0$ by matching power-series coefficients: the $s^2t^0$
coefficient kills $a$, the $s^3t^0$ coefficient kills $b$, and so on by
induction.  The linearity of $\mathcal{R}(u,0)$ is derived from the
functional equation, not assumed in the function class.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Composition-law forcing]\label{thm:rcl}
Among symmetric, right-affine combiners with natural boundary
conditions, the polynomial $2uv+2u+2v$ is the unique gate.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Condition 4: unit calibration.}
$d^2F(e^t)/dt^2|_{t=0}=1$.  The Hessian at the origin in
log-coordinates is one.  This fixes the only remaining freedom, the
multiplicative scale.

\begin{theorem}[Cost uniqueness]\label{thm:cost}
Any continuous $F:(0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ satisfying conditions 1--4
equals $\J$.
\end{theorem}

The proof reduces to d'Alembert's functional equation in
log-coordinates.  Acz\'el's classification~\cite{Aczel1966} of
continuous solutions of $g(s+t)+g(s-t)=2g(s)g(t)$ gives
$g(t)=\cosh(\mu t)$, and unit calibration fixes $\mu=1$.
Appendix~\ref{app:proofs} contains the sketch;
Washburn-Zlatanovi\'c~\cite{WashburnZlatanovic2026} contains the
measure-theoretic generalization.  The required smoothness is not an
extra hypothesis: it is supplied by a proved regularity package, so the
classification closes without assuming differentiability.

The local expansion
\begin{equation}\label{eq:expansion}
\J(e^t)=\frac{1}{2}t^2+\frac{1}{24}t^4+O(t^6)
\end{equation}
connects directly to gravity.  The quadratic term is the spin-two
kinetic contribution to the gravitational action; the quartic and
higher terms carry non-linear recognition corrections that vanish in
the continuum limit.  The expansion is even in $t$, as reciprocal
symmetry requires.

This ends the freedom to choose actions.  Any later parameter that
cannot be traced to $\J$ or to one of the four conditions is forbidden.
The gravitational sector, the quantum channel, and the cosmological
predictions all inherit their rigidity from this step.

\paragraph{What if a condition is dropped?}
Dropping any one condition opens a family of solutions.  Without
reciprocal symmetry, directional costs $F(x)\neq F(x^{-1})$ appear,
introducing a preferred comparison partner that gravity does not have.
Without normalization, a free constant offset becomes an undetermined
cosmological term at the action level.  Without the composition law,
the gate structure is free and the gravitational action coefficients
become adjustable.  Without calibration, the overall normalization of
$\J$ is free and the coupling constant becomes a fit parameter.  The
four conditions are not chosen to produce $\J$; $\J$ is what they
force.

\paragraph{Relation to information geometry.}
$\J$ is the ratio-space analogue of the Fisher-Rao metric: where
Chentsov's theorem singles out Fisher-Rao as the unique metric
invariant under sufficient statistics, the four comparison conditions
single out $\J$ on positive ratios.  This is an interpretive bridge,
not a separate theorem, and the paper claims no independent
Fisher-Rao-to-Einstein variational route.

\label{sec:chain}

The chain from the cost functional to the physical substrate is a

\begin{equation}\label{eq:chain-summary}

\;\Longrightarrow\;

\phiG\ \text{forced}
\;\Longrightarrow\;

\;\Longrightarrow\;

\;}
\end{equation}
Every arrow is a proved implication.  An earlier version of this chain
carried one external input at the dimension step, the first homology of
the circle.  That input has since been discharged: the circle's winding
structure is derived internally from the substrate's cyclic hierarchy,
through a geodesic-lift construction that generates the relevant cycle
classes and pins $H_1(S^1)\cong\mathbb{Z}$.  Nothing in
\eqref{eq:chain-summary} now cites an axiom beyond the comparison law
itself.  This is the single largest formal upgrade since the previous
version of this paper.

\paragraph{Why $\phiG=(1+\sqrt5)/2$.}
The cost functional is preserved under the unique non-trivial scale
map compatible with reciprocal symmetry and the composition law.  A
scale transformation $x\mapsto x^\lambda$ composes to
$x\mapsto x^{\lambda^2}$; requiring the scaled cost to satisfy the same
functional equation forces $\lambda^2=\lambda+1$, whose unique positive
root is $\phiG$.  The golden ratio enters because $x^2=x+1$ is the
minimal polynomial relating consecutive self-similar scales under the
forced composition.  Any other claimed scaling ratio would violate
either cost uniqueness or the gate structure.

\paragraph{Why $D=3$.}
Three spatial dimensions are forced by a linking argument.  The
substrate's conserved cyclic structures must be able to link without
intersecting; the obstruction that makes linking topologically
meaningful is the non-vanishing first homology of the circle, and the
dimension in which one-dimensional cycles generically link is three.
Below three dimensions the cycles intersect; above three they unlink.
The homology input is internal (see above), so the dimension theorem
now stands on the comparison law alone.  An independent consistency
check: the recognition period $2^D$ and the ledger gap $45$
synchronize at $\mathrm{lcm}(8,45)=360=2^3\cdot 3^2\cdot 5$, which
exists only for $D=3$.

\paragraph{Why eight ticks.}
Once the dimension theorem gives three spatial directions, the minimal
temporal cadence is $2^3=8$: one binary comparison branch per forced
spatial direction.  Fewer ticks cannot resolve all three branchings;
more introduce a binary degree of freedom not tied to any forced
direction.  The theorem order matters and runs dimension-first: three
dimensions, therefore eight ticks, not the reverse.

\paragraph{What the chain does not determine.}
The chain fixes the substrate geometry, the cadence, and the
gravitational action.  It does not fix the initial conditions of the
universe, the global topology of the spatial manifold (only the local
dimension), or the matter content, which enters through coupling to
the recognition action and is treated in companion papers on the
Standard Model spectrum.

\section{The Geometric Limit: The Discrete Recognition Action}
\label{sec:action}

The cost theorem forces $\J(x)=\cosh(\log x)-1$.  The natural signed
quantity derived from $\J$ is the recognition gradient
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sinh}
\frac{d}{dt}\J(e^t)\Big|_{t=\log x}=\sinh(\log x)=\frac{x-x^{-1}}{2},
\end{equation}
signed exactly as a gravitational action requires: positive curvature
contributes positively, negative curvature negatively.

On a four-dimensional simplicial manifold $\mathcal{T}_h$ with mesh
scale $h$, define the recognition action
\begin{equation}\label{eq:SRS}
S_\RS[\mathcal{T}_h]
=\frac{1}{8\pi G_\RS}\sum_{\sigma}A_\sigma\,\sinh(\delta_\sigma),
\end{equation}
with $A_\sigma$ the area of the codimension-2 hinge $\sigma$ and
$\delta_\sigma$ its signed deficit angle.  Expanding
$\sinh\delta=\delta+\delta^3/6+\cdots$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sinh-expand}
S_\RS=\underbrace{\frac{1}{8\pi G}\sum_\sigma
A_\sigma\delta_\sigma}_{=\,S_\Reg}
+\frac{1}{48\pi G}\sum_\sigma A_\sigma\delta_\sigma^{3}
+O(\delta^5).
\end{equation}
The leading term is exactly the Regge action~\cite{Regge1961}; the
cubic term carries the non-linear recognition content and is the
correction whose smallness the convergence analysis must control.

\begin{definition}[Admissible triangulation family]
A family $\mathcal{T}_h\to(M,g)$ is admissible if it satisfies a mesh
bound $\max_e \ell(e)\le h$, a shape-regularity bound, a curvature
bound $|\delta_\sigma|\le C_{\mathcal K}h^2$, and the
recognition-ratio condition $\log x_\sigma=\kappa_\sigma\delta_\sigma
+O(h^3)$, uniformly over a compact metric class with bounded curvature
derivatives, bounded inverse injectivity radius, and controlled
boundary data.
\end{definition}

The curvature bound makes the $\sinh$ expansion converge as the mesh
refines; the ratio condition ties the substrate's comparison data to
the geometric deficit.

\paragraph{Why $\sinh$ and not $\sin$ or linear.}
The gravitational action is built from the signed recognition
gradient, and the gradient of $\cosh-1$ is $\sinh$.  The $\cosh$
rather than $\cos$ is itself forced: the composition polynomial
$2uv+2u+2v$ grows without bound, which is compatible with $\cosh$ and
incompatible with the bounded, periodic $\cos$.  Unbounded growth is
essential physics: large curvature defects must carry large action.
In Euclidean or absolute-weight readouts this becomes large-curvature
suppression; in the Lorentzian path sum the ultraviolet control comes
from the substrate itself, as follows.

\begin{remark}[Ultraviolet finiteness of the recognition path sum]
\label{rem:uv}
The path sum
$Z=\sum_{T:\,h(T)\ge\ell_\bsub}\mu(T)\,
\exp(iS_\RS(T)/\hbar_\RS)$
is finite for three structural reasons.  The substrate mesh is bounded
below by $\ell_\bsub>0$, so no triangulation finer than the substrate
scale enters.  The number of combinatorially distinct triangulations
of a compact manifold with at most $N=\Vol(M)/\ell_\bsub^4$ simplices
is bounded by $C^N$, so the sum is finite.  And
$\sinh\delta\ge\delta$ for $\delta\ge 0$, so the recognition action
penalizes large positive deficits at least as strongly as the Regge
action.  The familiar perturbative divergences of continuum gravity
are artifacts of taking the mesh to zero while holding the metric
fixed; the substrate never takes that limit.  The continuum
Einstein-Hilbert action is an approximation to the discrete action
valid at $\ell\gg\ell_\bsub$, not the other way around.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{Matter and boundary.}
Boundary and volume completion target the full Einstein-Hilbert action
with the Gibbons-Hawking-York term,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:SEH}
S_\EH^{\partial}(g)=\frac{1}{16\pi G_\RS}\int_M(R_g-2\Lambda_\RS)
\sqrt{|g|}\,\dd^4x
+\frac{1}{8\pi G_\RS}\int_{\partial M}K_\gamma\sqrt{|\gamma|}\,\dd^3x .

The boundary term controls horizon thermodynamics: without it the

When the previous version of this paper went out, both inputs were

\begin{theorem}[Two-scale residual from the local cubic bound]

\frac{1}{1+K}\right),

K \;=\; \frac{|V|}{6}\,C\sum_\tau\|\xi_\tau\|^{3},

\begin{definition}[Recognition ledger]\label{def:ledger}

cell $i$ is $\Delta_i=\sum_j\mathcal{L}(i,j)$, and in the continuum

\end{definition}

The ledger unifies the three sectors of this paper: restricted to

\begin{definition}[Physical channel response]\label{def:physchannel}
A channel response arises from substrate access when it is obtained by
preparing a matter probe, applying the joint substrate dynamics,
reading a channel coordinate, and calibrating by a nonzero scalar.  A
response is \emph{density-only} when it is blind to global phase:
$R(c\,\psi)=R(\psi)$ for every unit-modulus $c$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Amplitude linearity is forced]\label{thm:amp}
Every channel response arising from substrate access is
complex-linear, because the joint substrate dynamics is complex-linear
and linear extraction preserves that structure.  A density-only
response that is also amplitude-linear is identically zero.
Consequently no nontrivial density-only mediator exists on the
substrate.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Many-body lift]\label{thm:manybody}
The forcing extends sitewise to the macroscopic ledger: on the tensor
product over lattice sites, the channel response built from per-site
linear witnesses is amplitude-linear, and pure product states evolve
sitewise.  The exclusion of density-only mediation therefore holds for
the many-body gravitational ledger, not only for a single register.
\end{theorem}

This is the load-bearing quantum-gravity claim, so we spell out the
algebra.  The density projection
$\pi(\psi)=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ maps the circle of states
$e^{i\theta}\psi_0+e^{i\phi}\psi_1$ to matrices depending only on
$\theta-\phi$, and a factorization $\Psi\circ\pi=\pi\circ U$ would
require one map $\Psi$ on densities to track the outputs of every
admissible amplitude update $U$.  Different updates imprint different
relative phases on the branches; no single density-level map can track
them all.  The obstruction is exact, dimension-independent, and
independent of any environment.  In dilation language: every
completely positive map has an isometric dilation, but here the
isometry is the primitive and the density-level channel is derived.  A
theory that starts from the density-level master equation and
reconstructs the isometry afterward has reversed the order of
explanation, and the no-go says the reversed order cannot reproduce
the comparison pairing.

predicts entanglement); what is specific here is that the amplitude
channel is forced by the comparison law rather than postulated, so a
clean null would falsify not a model choice but the framework's
central theorem about gravity.

\subsection{The vacuum ledger and the 122-decade problem}

\rho_{\Lambda,\RS}=\rho_\Pl\cdot\phiG^{-2s}.

The horizon is not a choice.  The ledger is defined on pairs of cells

future-directed knowledge, and the ledger records only comparisons

n=2s=588,\qquad
\phiG^{-588}=10^{-122.88},

\begin{equation}\label{eq:vacuum}
\rho_{\Lambda,\RS}=\rho_\Pl\,\phiG^{-588}
=6.7\times10^{-27}\ \text{kg/m}^3,
\end{equation}
within 14\% (0.06 dex) of observation, against a 122.9-decade naive
problem.  The residual traces to the input cosmological parameters

Two structural contrasts.  Weinberg's anthropic bound is consistent

the cosmological principle requires of $\rho_\Lambda$.

\subsection{The dynamic kernel}
\label{sec:kernel}

Distance-inferred reconstructions of $w(z)$ are a different
observable, and the framework forces their shape rather than leaving
it free.  The conserved cosmic-aging flow law
$(1+z)\bigl(w(z)+1\bigr)=\J(\phiG)$ has the unique solution
\begin{equation}\label{eq:kernel}
w(z)=-1+\frac{\J(\phiG)}{1+z},
\qquad
\J(\phiG)=\phiG-\tfrac32=0.1180\ldots,
\end{equation}
with no tunable coefficient.  The raw present-day amplitude
$\J(\phiG)$ is then attenuated by the recognition-aging dilution
$\theta=\phiG^{-4}$, so the observable deviation today is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:deltaw}
\delta w(0)=\phiG^{-4}\,\J(\phiG)=0.0172\ldots
\end{equation}
Mapped onto the standard two-parameter form
$w(a)=w_0+w_a(1-a)$, the kernel with attenuation lands on the single
point
\begin{equation}
(w_0,\,w_a)=\bigl(-1+\phiG^{-4}\J(\phiG),\ -\phiG^{-4}\J(\phiG)\bigr)
\approx(-0.983,\ -0.017),
\end{equation}
close to the cosmological-constant corner and far from the large
excursions some dataset combinations prefer.  The DESI Y1 indication

a falsifier or a confirmation: under the taxonomy of \S\ref{sec:eos}
it must be compared against the kernel row \eqref{eq:kernel}, not
against the static row, and its current significance and
sample-dependence leave both rows viable.  A stable reconstruction
inconsistent with both rows kills the dark-energy sector outright;
that is the sector's falsifier, and it is parameter-free on this side.

The kernel is not quintessence.  No scalar field rolls; the deviation
is recognition aging of the vacuum ledger, its shape is antitone in
redshift with fixed amplitude, and a measured thawing or freezing
trajectory of any other shape excludes it.

\subsection{Cost uniqueness}
Write $f(t)=F(e^t)$.  Reciprocal symmetry gives evenness; normalization
gives $f(0)=0$.  The composition law becomes
$f(s+t)+f(s-t)=2f(s)f(t)+2f(s)+2f(t)$; setting $g=1+f$ yields
d'Alembert's equation $g(s+t)+g(s-t)=2g(s)g(t)$.  Continuous solutions
are $g(t)=\cosh(\mu t)$ or $g\equiv1$~\cite{Aczel1966}; the trivial
solution fails calibration, and $f''(0)=1$ forces $\mu=1$.  Hence
$f(t)=\cosh t-1=\J(e^t)$.  The smoothness needed to run the
classification is supplied by a proved regularity package rather than
assumed.
